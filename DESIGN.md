# DESIGN.md — Training Hub (English Academy Center)

This design direction comes from the product owner (EAC Academic Operations). The agent only tidies it up.

## Identity
- Product: Training Hub for English Academy Center (By Ruangguru).
- Audience: trainees, new teachers, new staff, and existing teachers/staff who want to refresh.
- Character: professional but warm — a place to learn that is serious yet friendly, not stiff.

## Palette
- Primary purple:   #6B5BDF   (deep #5142BF, soft #EFEBFF)
- Ink / navy-dark:  #1E1B4B   (strong text accent, footer)
- Page background:  #F3EFFF   (soft lavender)
- Coral accent:     #F0646B   reserved for single key moments (matches the red in the EAC chat-bubble logo)
- Cards: white, with 20–24px radius and soft shadows.
- Layout inspired by modern three-zone dashboards: a collapsible sidebar with a pill-shaped active item, a hero greeting banner, stat cards, and a right-hand rail.

## Typography
- Feel: round, friendly, easy to read.
- Choice: "Nunito" (round, warm, good Indonesian-language support) for headings & body. Reason: its rounded letterforms fit the warm mood, and it's highly readable for learning material.

## Mood / Dials (Liveliness)
- ENERGY 2 (balanced — a clear greeting but not over the top)
- RHYTHM 2 (consistent, with some variation between sections)
- MOTION 1 (only subtle hover/transitions — this is a learning tool, not a stage)

## Identity motif
- The brand mark is the EAC chat bubble (`assets/logo-ea.png`) next to the words "Training Hub".
- The "chat bubble" from the EAC logo is a small motif that recurs (not a generic emoji).
- The coral accent appears at only one key moment per screen.

## Constraints (per Anti-Slop)
- Don't imitate other products (e.g. the "Donezo" dashboard layout). The identity comes from the EAC logo.
- Don't show numbers/statistics/testimonials/user profiles that aren't real.
- Icons must be relevant to the content, not decoration.
- Logo: use the real EAC logo file; if it isn't on the web yet, show the name as text (an honest placeholder).
